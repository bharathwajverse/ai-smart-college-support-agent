Bayesian Escalation Model

P(E|Evidence)

Computes the posterior escalation probability using Bayesian inference by combining historical departmental priors with observed evidence factors:

P(Escalation | D, U, R) = α · P(Escalation | Category) · ∏ P(Evidence | Escalation)

  • • Urgency Lexical Markers: P(U | E) = 0.80
  • • Recurring Cluster Incident: P(R | E) = 0.75
  • • Prior Probabilities: Anti-Ragging (0.85), Mess (0.50), Hostel (0.35), IT (0.25)
